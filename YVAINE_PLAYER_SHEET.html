📖 The Fall of Solmere & Detective Arc
Eldest princess of the white-stone kingdom of Solmere. On her 13th birthday, the palace gates were unlatched from the inside, the royal guard dismissed on forged orders, and her parents slaughtered in their bedchambers.

Yvaine never saw the traitor's face—only the shadow of someone who knew the royal codes by heart. She buried her parents with a servant's spade, took her father's rifle "Lament", and spent 7 years in the undercity forging herself into an apex Monster Slayer hunting her missing younger sisters, Vesper and Liora. In Barovia, she operates in full detective mode, tracking occult signs to uncover the traitor's identity and prove her sisters are safe.
🎭 Persona, Ideals & Flaws
• Ideals: Family (sisters before kingdoms), Ruthless Accountability (traitors do not get trials).
• Flaws: Hyper-Vigilance (checks chamber seals compulsively), Guilt (believes she should have died instead).
