⚖️ SECTION 5: BAROVIAN GOTHIC LAWS & TABLE HOUSE RULES GLANCEABLE TABLE RULINGS
🚪 1. Vampire Forbiddance LawCrucial Lore

Neither Strahd nor any Vampire Spawn can enter a private residence without an explicit invitation from a living occupant. They must lure, charm, or drag PCs outside!

☁️ 2. Overcast Sky vs True SunlightBarovian Sky

Barovia's natural daytime light is muted by perpetual storm clouds and does NOT trigger vampire sunlight damage. Only the Sunsword, Holy Symbol, and Dawn create true sunlight.

💀 3. 2-Strike Death Saves & Dark WhispersTrilogy Hook

1 Success stabilizes at 1 HP; 2 Failures = Death. In Barovia, a dying PC hears the Dark Powers whisper: accept a Dark Gift / Corruption Mark to rise immediately at 1 HP!

💥 4. Brutal Critical HitsHouse Rule

On a Natural 20, deal Maximum Base Die + 1 Normal Die Roll + Ability Modifier (e.g., 1d8 Rapier crit = 8 + 1d8 + Mod).

🧪 5. Potions & Short RestsAction Economy

Potion of Healing: Bonus Action self (2d6 + 1 HP), Action to feed ally.
Short Rest (Max 2/day): Heals 25% Max HP + regains 1 Help Token.

🤝 6. Help Tokens (+1d6) & CoverTactical Rules

Help Tokens (2/2): Spend 1 token with narrative justification to add +1d6 to an ally's d20 roll.
Cover: Partial Cover = +2 AC • Total Cover = Untargetable.
