📜 The Four Sacred Laws of Barovia

1. Law of Forbiddance: Vampires cannot enter private homes without an explicit invitation from a living occupant. (Taverns, shops, churches & ruined manors do not count!)

2. Law of the Overcast Sky: Barovia's natural daytime is filtered through perpetual storm clouds and fog—it never triggers Vampire Sunlight Hypersensitivity (only The Sunsword, Holy Symbol of Ravenkind, and Dawn spell do!).

3. Law of Running Water: Ending a turn in running river water (Ivlis River, Tser Falls) deals 20 Acid damage and suppresses vampire regeneration.

4. Law of the Mists (Dark Whisper): 2 failed Death Saves in Act I–II triggers a split-second time freeze: a voice in the Mists offers revival at 1 HP in exchange for accepting a permanent Dark Power Touched Mark!

5. Law of the Frozen Second (Derek's Blind Spot): Whenever Derek (Avatar of the Dark Powers) visits (tap, tap, tap), time freezes completely. Because he walks strictly between the ticks of the clock, Count Strahd, his spies, and Barovian NPCs have ZERO knowledge that Derek exists!

🎲 Universal Table House Rules

Brutal Crits (Nat 20): Max Base Die + 1 Rolled Die + Modifier (automatically calculated when you roll a Nat 20 in the monster window!).

2-Strike Death Saves: 1 Success stabilizes at 1 HP (prone). 2 Failures = Death (or Dark Power Bargain).

Drinking Potions: Bonus Action for self (2d6 + 1 HP); Action to feed a downed ally.

Short Rest Healing: Restores 25% of Max HP (up to 2/day) and regains +1 Help Token (max 2/2).

2-Coin Economy: 10 Silver Pieces (SP) = 1 Gold Piece (GP).
