## 👑 PROLOGUE: THE DUAL-POV COLD OPEN

*(Pacing: 0:00 – 0:25 | Ambience: Dim room lights, warm hearth tones before thunder)*

### 1. Jacomir's Royal Spotlight (Oakenshire)
Turn to Jacomir’s player:
> *"Jacomir, we open on you. The Great Hall of Oakenshire is packed wall-to-wall with your subjects. Gilded banners hang from the high stone rafters, your royal vanguard stands at rigid attention with halberds gleaming, and hundreds of men and women are chanting your name. The war is won. The realm is at peace. The court falls silent as all eyes turn up to the dais.*
> 
> *Your Majesty... the floor is yours. Deliver your victory speech."*

* **DM Instruction:** Let Jacomir deliver his speech uninterrupted. Let him bask in the majesty and adoration of his kingdom.

---

### 2. The Sensory Sever (The Ringing & The Horrific Smiles)
As soon as his speech winds down or hits its climax, lean in and lower your voice:

> *"The applause is thunderous. Goblets clink, cheers rattle the stained glass... and then, Jacomir, a high-pitched, piercing ring hits your right ear.*
> 
> *It's sharp. Like a hot silver needle driven into your temple. You raise a hand to your brow, but the ringing grows louder—and the cheering of your court begins to muffle, sounding distant and hollow, like you've been submerged under thirty feet of cold water.*
> 
> *You look down at your boots. Creeping from beneath the arched double doors across the warm polished marble is a low, freezing white mist. It isn't smoke from the hearths—it smells of damp grave-dirt and dead pines."*

* **🎯 DM PROMPT — CALL FOR CHECK:**  
  **"Jacomir, give me a Perception check."**  
  *(DC 10 | Jacomir has +3 and Passive 13 — automatic success)*

> *"Through the rising fog, you scan the front rows.*
> 
> *Your nobles are still clapping in slow, soundless motion... but in the fourth row, a nobleman in velvet stands completely rigid. Beside him, an armored guard of your vanguard does not blink. And a young girl holding a bouquet of flowers stands frozen.*
> 
> *None of them are clapping. None of them are breathing. Their heads are tilted at an unnatural, identical angle, their dead eyes locked directly onto yours.*
> 
> *And as the freezing gray fog reaches your chest and curls around your throat... their lips slowly, unnervingly begin to pull back.*
> 
> *Wider. Wider than a human mouth should stretch. A grotesque, ear-to-ear, mocking grin.*
> 
> *You blink—"*

## 🔥 ACT I: THE CARRIAGE & THE HIGH-SPEED CHASE

*(Pacing: 0:25 – 0:55 | High Adrenaline Survival & Rescue)*

### 🎙️ The Violent Eruption (`ROLL INITIATIVE!`)
Cut off their argument violently:

> *"Before either of you can pull a trigger or draw steel—*
> 
> *A bloodcurdling scream rips from the carriage roof!*
> 
> *SLAP! Something wet and heavy smacks against the glass between you: the severed arm of the coachman, trailing ragged muscle, still gripping a snapped leather rein!*
> 
> *With no driver at the helm, the two black draft stallions go into a blind panic, stampeding down a narrow gorge beside a two-hundred-foot cliff! Glowing crimson pinpricks pace the coach through the black pines, and with a deafening CRUNCH, a horse-sized Svalich Dire Wolf shatters through the left carriage door in a spray of splintered wood and fangs!*
> 
> ***JACOMIR, YVAINE—ROLL INITIATIVE!"***

---

### 🚨 Phase 1: The Cabin Breach & The Driver's Box Choice
* **Encounter State:** **1x Svalich Dire Wolf** (`AC 14, 37 HP`) is wedged in the left door frame, snapping inside (`+5 hit, 2d6+3 piercing + DC 13 STR save or Prone`).
* **The Dilemma (Seize Reins vs. Jump!):**
  * The horses are running blind toward a cliff edge. Someone must get into the driver's box outside to take the reins, OR they have to bail!
  * **Option A: Seize the Reins:** A character uses an action and makes a **DC 11 Athletics or Acrobatics check** to scramble through the broken front window onto the bloody driver's box and grab the reins.
  * **Option B: Jump Out:** If a player suggests jumping, remind them: *"The carriage is moving at thirty-five miles an hour, and outside in the dark are twenty pairs of glowing crimson eyes. Humans run at 30 feet; dire wolves sprint at 50!"* (Staying with the carriage is their only survival chance).

---

### 🎭 Phase 2: Camera Pan to Piper (Solo Spotlight)
As soon as the first wolf in the cabin is bloodied or the reins are seized, cut the camera! Look away from Jacomir and Yvaine and point directly at **Piper**:

*(DM Background Context: Ever since her nightmare in the woods violently awakened her Wild Magic, Piper has been privately traveling toward **Tser Pool** to find **Madam Eva**—the ancient Vistani seer rumored to be able to pierce the Mists and find lost souls. She believes Madam Eva can reveal where her parents are! She was on the road when the wolves wrecked her cart. She keeps this secret from Jacomir and Yvaine for now, but it is why her path naturally aligns with the party).*

> *"Meanwhile, three hundred yards down the mountain road... Piper.*
> 
> *Your traveling carnival cart is completely wrecked on its side in the ditch. Colorful silks, playing cards, and broken mirrors are scattered into the mud.*
> 
> *You are backed up against a sheer rock cliff. Three Svalich Dire Wolves are circling you, their hackles raised, snapping jaws dripping with saliva, their eyes burning with unnatural crimson witchlight. The alpha wolf's muscles coil—and it LEAPS straight for your throat!*
> 
> ***Piper: Give me a Dexterity Saving Throw!"***

* **🎯 DM MECHANIC — PIPER'S DEX SAVE (DC 12):**
  * **Success (12+):** Piper ducks, rolls under the broken axle of her cart, or snaps her fingers to blast a shower of sparks into the wolf's eyes! The wolf smashes face-first into the cart timber.
  * **Failure (<12):** She dodges just in time, but the wolf's claws scrape across her shoulder (`1d6` glancing damage), sending her tumbling into the mud.
  * **Either Way:** She scrambles up and hears the roar of iron wheels and panicked horses barreling down the road straight toward her!

---

### 🤝 Phase 3: The Merge ("Do You Save Her?!")
Pan your eyes back to **Jacomir and Yvaine**:

> *"Through the rain and lightning, you see an overturned wagon with broken mirrors and cards blowing in the wind.*
> 
> *Backed up against the rock is a young tiefling woman with purple horns, holding off three snarling dire wolves with nothing but a cane and sparks. The carriage is hurtling straight toward her at breakneck speed.*
> 
> ***Jacomir, Yvaine: Do you swerve past her... or do you try to save her?!"***

* **When they choose to save her:**
  1. **The Driver:** Makes a **DC 11 Animal Handling / Vehicle check** to drift the carriage close to the embankment.
  2. **The Rescuer:** Leans out the door or reaches down an arm/weapon to pull her in.
  3. **Piper:** Makes a **DC 10 Acrobatics check** (or casts *Mage Hand*, *Misty Step*, or an athletic leap) to scramble onto the rear brass luggage rail!
* **The Table is Merged!** Piper scrambles through the rear window into the cabin, breathless and mud-splattered:
  > *"PULL ME IN! PULL ME IN! THANK YOU—DON'T STOP DRIVING!"*
* Piper rolls into Initiative alongside Jacomir and Yvaine!

---

### 🏁 Phase 4: The 3-Round Cinematic Chase

Run this using the **"One Success Keeps You Moving" Rule**:
> **⚡ DM TABLE RULE:** In each round, all three players describe an action (Attack, Spell, or Skill Check). If **at least ONE player succeeds**, the team beats that hazard! If a player fails their individual roll, they suffer a **Personal Complication** (small scrape damage, jammed weapon, or lost prop).

```
ROUND 1: Rear Pursuit (Shake the pack)
ROUND 2: The Three-Point Surround (Left, Right & Roof Breach!)
ROUND 3: The Dead End at the Locked Gates (Drift-slide crash)
```

#### Round 1: Rear Pursuit
* **The Hazard:** Two dire wolves chase the rear bumper, snapping at Piper's boots and trying to chew through the rear axle!
* **Player Prompts:**
  * *Yvaine:* Fires her pistols at the pursuers (`Attack vs AC 14`).
  * *Jacomir:* Leans out to slash with his Greatsword or braces the rear door with *Athletics*.
  * *Piper:* Casts *Fire Bolt* (`+6 hit`) or *Minor Illusion* to create phantom fire.
* **Result:** At least 1 success scatters the rear wolves!

#### Round 2: The Three-Point Surround (Left, Right & Roof Breach!)
* **The Hazard:** The pack surges forward on all sides!
  > *"The storm howls louder, and the pack surrounds the carriage!*
  > 
  > *Through the broken left window, a massive dire wolf runs alongside you, snapping at Jacomir's elbow! On the right window, a second wolf matches your speed, ramming the door!*
  > 
  > *And then—SLAM!*
  > 
  > *The carriage groans as something massive lands directly on the roof above you! Plaster and splinters shower down onto your faces as four-inch black claws punch straight through the ceiling planks, tearing away the wood to get inside!*
  > 
  > ***Left window, right window, and roof. You're completely surrounded! What do you do?!"***
* **Player Action Options:**
  * **Attacking the Roof Wolf:** Jacomir thrusts his greatsword upward through the ceiling; Yvaine fires point-blank into the rafters; Piper blasts *Chaos Bolt* through the hole (`Attack vs AC 14`).
  * **Defending the Windows:** Slashing or blasting the flankers off the doors.
  * **The Driver's Crazy Maneuver (Environmental Solution):** The driver rolls **DC 12 Animal Handling or Athletics** to deliberately sideswipe the carriage against the rocky cliff, scraping the roof wolf off on an overhanging branch!
* **Result:** 1 success repels the breach! A failure deals `1d6` claw damage to that individual player.

#### Round 3: The Dead End at the Locked Gates!
* **The Hazard:** Ahead in the lightning stand two 30-foot stone statues flanking the massive rusted **Gates of Barovia**—and they are **LOCKED SHUT**!
  > *"Ahead through the deluge, the road ends at two colossal headless stone statues flanking a towering pair of rusted iron gates—and they are slammed completely shut! If you hit them at this speed, the carriage will shatter into toothpicks!*
  > 
  > ***Driver: Slam the brakes! Pull the reins!"***
* **The Driving Check:** The driver makes a **DC 12 Animal Handling or Athletics check**.
* **The Cinematic Crash:** 
  > *"You haul back on the reins with all your strength! The horses shriek, rearing up as the carriage violently fishtails sideways across the mud!*
  > 
  > *CRACK! The rear axle snaps against a boulder with a sound like a cannon shot! The carriage rolls onto its side, skidding twenty feet through the freezing mud before slamming to a halt against a mossy stone milestone—**twenty feet in front of the locked iron gates**!*
  > 
  > *You scramble out of the wreckage into the freezing rain, bruised and breathless, your backs against the locked iron bars.*
  > 
  > *Out of the dark treeline behind you, the entire pack of dire wolves emerges—twenty pairs of crimson eyes, snarling, cutting off the road. You are completely cornered."*

## 🚪 ACT II: THE GATES OF BAROVIA & THE LOST MESSENGER

*(Pacing: 0:55 – 1:30 | 35 Minutes | The Silent Command, The Iron Threshold & Dalvan's Compass)*

*(Architecture Note: Faithfully following DragnaCarta's Curse of Strahd: Reloaded — Arc B: Svalich Woods Standard).*

### 🎙️ 1. The Silent Command & The Opening Gates
The party raises weapons for a bloody last stand in the mud. Then, cut the sound completely:

> *"The wolves stalk toward you in a slow, suffocating semicircle, bellies scraping the mud, teeth bared. You raise your weapons for a final stand.*
> 
> *And then... the thunder stops.*
> 
> *The howling wind abruptly drops into a dead, unnatural silence. The freezing rain turns to a soundless, hanging mist.*
> 
> *Every single wolf in the pack freezes. Mid-step. Their snarling dies in their throats. Their ears pin back.*
> 
> *As one unified body, all twenty wolves lower their heads and drop flat into the wet earth. They do not look at you. They turn their crimson eyes up toward the towering precipice in the distance—where the jagged black spires of Castle Ravenloft pierce the stormy heavens like broken daggers.*
> 
> *Without making a sound, the wolves rise, turn in synchronized military formation, and melt silently backward into the choking fog.*
> 
> *Behind you—SCREEEEEECH.*
> 
> *The colossal thirty-foot iron gates begin to move. Rusted chains rattle deep within the stone pillars. Without a living soul touching them, the massive iron doors groan open on silent hinges, swinging outward into the black woods beyond.*
> 
> *High atop the stone archway, perched on the moss-covered shoulder of a headless stone guardian, sits a solitary, pitch-black raven. Its feathers are sleek against the rain. Its eyes catch the pale gloom with an intelligent, silver starlight glimmer. It tilts its head, watching you with uncanny patience."*

*(DM Secret Note: This is **Cael** in Wild Shape! He has followed the carriage through the storm, studying these three newcomers to assess their strength before they enter the valley. See [`CAEL_MASTER_DOSSIER.md`](file:///D:/Dungeons%20and%20Dragons%20Campaigns/CURSE%20OF%20STRAHD/CAEL_MASTER_DOSSIER.md)).*

### 🚪 2. Crossing the Threshold (The Dimensional Cage Closes)
Look at Jacomir, Yvaine, and Piper:

> *"The gates stand wide open before you. Ahead, the cobblestone road disappears into a valley shrouded in perpetual twilight and suffocating pine trees. Behind you, the mountain road you just descended is swallowed in a wall of opaque, freezing white fog.*
> 
> ***Do you step across the threshold?"***

* When the party steps through the gateway:
  > *"The moment your boots cross the iron threshold—*
  > 
  > *SLAM!*
  > 
  > *With a deafening, bone-shaking BOOM, the thirty-foot iron gates slam shut behind you! The rusted latch drops into place with the finality of a prison cell door. The sound echoes for miles through the dead pines.*
  > 
  > *You are inside Barovia."*

### 🔍 3. Dalvan Olensky & The Spinning Compass (*DragnaCarta Reloaded*)
Twenty paces past the gate, caught in the brambles beside a massive ancient pine tree, Yvaine's sharp eyes or Jacomir's military instinct spots something in the weeds:

> *"Off the right shoulder of the road, half-buried under soggy pine needles and thorny briars, lies a figure face-down in the mud.*
> 
> *It is a human man dressed in the worn wool cloak and leather boots of a Barovian traveler. His pack lies torn beside him, his garments shredded by thorns."*

#### 🩺 The Examination (DC 10 Medicine Check):
* Any character inspecting the corpse makes a **DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check**:
  * **Success:** They discover a chilling physiological fact: **There are no bite marks, no claw lacerations, and no stab wounds.** His neck is unbroken. His lips are cracked and blue; his skin is papery and gray. **He died purely of severe exhaustion, hypothermia, and dehydration.** He ran until his heart gave out.

#### 🧭 The Clues: The Compass & The 13 Tally Marks:
* **The Frozen Hand:** The dead man’s right hand is stiff and splayed out in the mud, clutching a **tarnished copper pocket compass**.
* **The Pointer:** His stiff index finger points directly toward the trunk of the towering pine tree five feet away.
* **The Carvings on the Pine Tree:**
  * Carved deeply into the bark with a hunting knife are **thirteen fresh tally marks** arranged in rows: `|||| |||| |||`.
  * Beneath the thirteenth mark, an arrow is carved pointing down an overgrown footpath that veers east into the thick, choking fog.

#### ⚠️ The Compass Mechanics & Testing the Perimeter:
* When a player picks up Dalvan's compass:
  > *"You wipe the muddy glass of the compass. The brass needle trembles... and then begins to spin.*
  > 
  > *It doesn't point north. It spins in lazy, erratic, dizzying circles, wobbling violently before tumbling backward. In this land, magnetic north does not exist."*
* **If Jacomir or Yvaine tests the footpath / tries to turn back to escape:**
  * If they follow Dalvan's footpath into the fog attempting to find a way out of the valley:
  * Describe the fog growing unnaturally thick, freezing cold, smelling of rotting lilacs and grave-dirt. Vision drops to five feet.
  * After ten minutes of marching along the path, the fog parts... and they step **right back out onto the Old Svalich Road, staring directly at Dalvan's corpse and the thirteen tally marks!**
  * If they attempt to push aggressively through the mist wall:
    * **DC 15 Constitution Saving Throw:** On a failure, the freezing fog seeps into their lungs, inflicting **1 level of Exhaustion** as it leaches their vitality.
* **The Crushing Realization (Jacomir & Yvaine's Reality Check):**
  * Point directly at Jacomir and Yvaine:
  > *"You look at the thirteen tally marks. You look at Dalvan's frozen, unbitten corpse. You look at the spinning needle.*
  > 
  > *Dalvan ran this loop thirteen times. He tried thirteen times to walk out of these woods... and the mists simply turned him in circles until he dropped dead in the brambles.*
  > 
  > *There is no walking home. There is no sailing back. You cannot leave Barovia through the woods. The only road out lies forward... through the ruler of this domain."*

### 📜 4. Piper's Backstory Hook: The Kolyan Plea & The Tarokka Fragment
Searching Dalvan's stiff leather satchel reveals three items:
1. **15 Silver Pieces** and a small flask of strong, sour Barovian plum brandy (*Tuica*).
2. **The Sealed Letter of Kolyan Indirovich:** Marked with the official wax crest of the Barovian Raven.
   > *"Hail to thee of might and valor:*
   > 
   > *I, a humble servant of the township of Barovia, plead for thy desperately needed assistance!*
   > 
   > *A terrifying darkness has descended upon our home. My adopted daughter, Ireena Kolyana, has been afflicted by an unnameable evil that our local militia cannot withstand. For six nights, the dead have battered our walls, and she languishes from terrible wounds.*
   > 
   > *I beg of you: hasten to my manor in the village. Help us protect her before the darkness claims her soul. All the wealth of House Indirovich shall be thine!*
   > 
   > *Kolyan Indirovich, Burgomaster"*
3. **The Tarokka Fragment (Piper's Spotlight):**
   * Tucked into Dalvan's breast pocket, wrapped in oiled cloth, is a single frayed, hand-painted playing card: **THE HORSEMAN** (depicting an armored knight on a skeletal black steed riding through dead trees).
   * Written across the bottom in elegant Vistani charcoal script is the inscription: *"Flee not the east, runner, for the pale rider waits in the pines."*
   * **Piper recognizes this immediately:**
     > *"Piper: You recognize the card stock, the charcoal ink, and the distinctive silk ribbon. This is a Tarokka card from Madam Eva's own personal deck.*
     > 
     > *Dalvan didn't just run blindly—before he fled the village, he went to the Vistani encampment at Tser Pool. Madam Eva drew this card for him... and she predicted his exact death in these woods.*
     > 
     > *If Madam Eva can see a mortal's demise with such terrifying precision, she is the only seer alive who can pierce the Mists and tell you the truth about your awakening magic and the fate of your parents."*
* Piper now has an undeniable, grounded reason to guide the party toward the village and onward to **Tser Pool**!

### 🪢 Scene 1: The River Ivlis Gallows (The Psychic Mirror)
*(Runtime: 1:30 – 1:50 | 20 Minutes)*

Following the Old Svalich Road through the ancient black pines, the road splits at a mossy stone signpost pointing toward the *Village of Barovia* to the east and *Tser Pool* to the northwest.

#### 🎙️ Read-Aloud Narration: The Gallows at the Fork
> *"The freezing rain slows to a miserable, bone-chilling drizzle. The black pines loom overhead like rotting cathedral spires, draped in sickly gray moss.*
> 
> *Ahead, the road widens at a muddy fork beside a rushing river. Standing on a low grass mound at the center of the crossroads is a rickety wooden gallows, its crossbeam soaked black with rot.*
> 
> *Hanging from a frayed hemp rope, turning slowly in the freezing mountain wind with a sickening rhythmic creak... is a human corpse.*
> 
> *Its head hangs limp. Black ravens perch on the crossbeam, watching you with intelligent, unblinking eyes."*

#### 🎭 The Horror Illusion (The Personal Mirror)
When a character approaches within 15 feet to inspect the corpse:
* Point directly at **Jacomir** (or **Yvaine**):
  > *"A gust of freezing wind catches the hanging body, spinning it slowly toward you.*
  > 
  > *The ragged hood slips back. The rotting, bruised face hanging from that noose... is not a stranger.*
  > 
  > ***It is your own face.***
  > 
  > *Your eyes stare back at you—milky white, dead, cheeks gaunt and gray. And as you freeze in terror, the jaw of your hanging duplicate slowly unhinges... pulling back into that exact same, grotesque, ear-to-ear grin from your throne room in Oakenshire."*

#### 🎯 DM Mechanics & Skill Checks:
* **The Target Rolls:** **DC 12 Wisdom Saving Throw**.
  * **Failure:** Overcome with chilling dread; gains the **Frightened** condition for 1 minute, taking `1d4` psychic dread damage.
  * **Success:** Shakes off the planar vision; blinks hard, and the illusion snaps—the corpse on the rope is just a withered, anonymous Barovian criminal who hanged months ago.
* **Investigation Check (DC 13):** Yvaine discovers the criminal's pockets contain an empty wooden token with a raven symbol and a handful of dead hemlock root (he chose hanging over what lurks in the forest).
* **Piper’s Barovian Lore:** Piper explains to the outsiders:
  > *"That's the Mirror of Ivlis. The Mists test your mind at every crossroads. If you give in to the fear, this land eats you from the inside out."*

### 🥧 Scene 2: Morgantha & The Dream Pastries (The Moral Rot)
*(Runtime: 1:50 – 2:10 | 20 Minutes)*

Approaching the outskirts of the village, the dead smell of damp pine suddenly gives way to an intoxicating, mouth-watering aroma: warm butter, cinnamon, spiced apples, and baked pastry crust.

#### 🎙️ Read-Aloud Narration: The Pie Cart in the Fog
> *"Through the swirling fog on the edge of the village comes the squeak of a wooden cartwheel.*
> 
> *Emerging from the mist is a hunched old woman wrapped in a patchwork shawl and a stained apron. She pushes a two-wheeled wooden cart with a chimney venting fragrant, savory steam into the freezing drizzle.*
> 
> *Her hair is a wild nest of gray, her face wrinkled like dried leather, but her voice is as sweet as honey:*
> 
> *'Pastries! Warm, sweet dream pastries! Warm your bellies, traveler dearies! Just one copper for a taste of heaven!'*
> 
> *She stops her cart, beaming at you with a gap-toothed smile, lifting a checkered cloth to reveal golden, flaky pastries that smell so good your stomachs rumble in unison."*

#### 👤 NPC Profile: Morgantha ("Granny")
* **Appearance:** A frail, grandmotherly peddler with dirt under her nails, smelling of cloves and brown sugar. (Secretly: The Coven Mother of the Night Hags of Old Bonegrinder in human guise!).
* **Her Offer:** Sells *Dream Pastries* for **1 Silver Piece** (or 5 Copper if haggled).
  * **The Mechanic:** Eating a pastry puts a character into an 8-hour euphoric, waking dream state of pure joy, granting **`1d6` Temporary Hit Points**. However, it is intensely addictive, and repeated use induces lethargy and nightmare cravings.
* **Piper’s Warning:** Piper immediately steps between the party and the cart:
  > *"Do NOT eat those. People in Barovia sell their last family heirlooms for those pies... and then they sell things far worse."*

#### 🚨 The Shocking Horror Reveal (The Child Trade)
Before the party can decide whether to buy:
1. Morgantha wheels her cart to a rundown cottage ten paces away. She raps three times with a wooden ladle.
2. A gaunt, hollow-eyed Barovian man and weeping mother open the door. They have no coin.
3. **🎯 Perception / Insight Check (DC 13):**
   * *Success:* Through the half-open door, Yvaine or Jacomir watches the weeping mother hand over a **wriggling burlap sack** containing their 7-year-old son, **Lucian**. The boy’s muffled sobs come from inside the sack.
   * Morgantha smiles warmly, pats the sack, sets **three warm pastries** on their windowsill, and shoves the wriggling sack into the bottom crate of her cart!
4. **The Confrontation (Moral Dilemma):**
   * If the party confronts her: Morgantha tut-tuts sweetly:
     > *"A fair trade, dearies! The parents get peace and sweet dreams; little Lucian comes with Granny to live in a warm windmill with all the sweets he can eat! You wouldn't deny a starving family their bread, would you, my handsome lords?"*
   * If attacked: Morgantha’s eyes flare sulfur-yellow. She reveals claws for a split second, sneers, and steps backward into the fog, **vanishing into the Ethereal Plane** with a terrifying cackle: *"I'll see you at the Old Bonegrinder, little birds!"* (Leaving behind the sack with the terrified boy).

>

### 🍷 Scene 5: The Blood on the Vine Tavern (Ismark's Pact & The Two Bites)
*(Runtime: 2:35 – 2:55 | 20 Minutes)*

The party arrives at the center of the village. The only warm light spills from a large, sagging two-story tavern with dirty leaded-glass windows: **Blood on the Vine**.

> Sitting at a large round table by the fire are three handsome women in vibrant silk scarves, gold hoop earrings, and embroidered vests, drinking blood-red wine and playing cards—VISTANI.
> 
> Behind the bar stands the barkeep: ARIK. He endlessly wipes the same filthy ceramic mug with a gray rag. His skin is waxy, his eyes dull and glassy. He looks up at you with zero curiosity, zero fear, and zero emotion. An empty, soulless shell."*

#### 👤 The Soulless Lore (Worldbuilding Beat)
* Piper explains to Jacomir and Yvaine:
  > *"Only one in ten people born in Barovia possesses a true soul. The rest are phantoms of the Mist... hollow shells formed from the dirt of the valley. When they die, they simply dissolve into the gray fog."*
* **Jacomir’s Realization:** Jacomir realizes why his court in Oakenshire looked so horrifyingly hollow—the Mists have been harvesting and mimicking living beings for centuries.

#### 💬 Ismark's Desperate Pact:
Ismark brings a flagon of *Purple Grapemash No. 3* to their table, leaning in close:

1. **The Truth of the Letter:**
   > *"You found Dalvan's body. He was our bravest runner. My father sent him to find heroes beyond the mists... but the Devil's fog turned him in circles until he died. 
   > 
   > My father, Burgomaster Kolyan, died three nights ago, friends. His heart burst from the terror of the siege."*
2. **The Horror of the Two Bites:**
   > *"Count Strahd has laid claim to my adopted sister, Ireena. 
   > 
   > He calls her 'Tatyana'—a name from centuries past. For six nights, his wolves and rotting corpses have battered our town and our manor. 
   > 
   > Twice already, the Count has breached our home. Twice he has looked into her eyes, charmed her will, and drunk the blood from her throat. 
   > 
   > In Barovia, the law of the vampire is absolute: on the **third bite**, the victim's heart turns to ice, and she rises as his undead bride for eternity."*
3. **The Mission & The Bargain:**
   > *"My father's body sits in a hammered pine box in our parlor. For three days, no priest or gravedigger has dared enter our home. 
   > 
   > I cannot leave my father to rot like carrion. And I cannot protect Ireena alone when midnight strikes.
   > 
   > Walk with me to the manor. Help me defend my sister through the night, and help me carry my father's coffin to Father Donavich's church for a consecrated burial at dawn. 
   > 
   > After that, I will give you every silver coin in my family's vault, and together we will escort Ireena to the fortified walled town of **Vallaki**, where the Devil cannot reach her. On the way, we pass **Tser Pool**, where Madam Eva can read your cards and reveal your path home. 
   > 
   > Will you stand with House Kolyanovich?"*

* When Jacomir, Yvaine, and Piper agree, Ismark drains his goblet, belts his longsword, and leads them through the dark streets toward the **Indirovich Mansion** as the clock approaches midnight!

## 🕯️ ACT IV: THE BESIEGED MANOR & THE MIDNIGHT PROMISE

### 🎙️ Read-Aloud Narration: The Indirovich Estate

> The estate looks like a fortress under siege. The front yard is churned into black mud, littered with scorched patches and dead wolf carcasses. Every ground-floor window is boarded shut with heavy, iron-banded timber planks. Deep claw marks gouge the heavy front doors.
> 
> Ismark steps up to the door and knocks: three short raps, a pause, then two heavy raps.
>

### 🌹 Meeting Ireena Kolyana
> *"The heavy oak door opens a crack, then swings wide.
> 
> Standing in the foyer with a drawn silver-hilted rapier held with practiced discipline is IREENA KOLYANA. 
> 
> She is a striking young woman in her early twenties with fiery auburn hair pulled back, pale skin, and fierce, sleepless resolve burning in her hazel eyes. Over her dark mourning dress, she wears a fitted steel breastplate.
> 
> Wrapped tightly around her neck is a high silk scarf, faintly concealing two dark, bruised puncture marks.
> 
> Behind her in the dim drawing room, resting atop two wooden sawhorses and surrounded by dozens of guttering white wax candles, is a simple, heavy pine coffin covered in wilting black roses: the corpse of Burgomaster Kolyan Indirovich."*

#### 👤 Deep NPC Profile: Ireena Kolyana
* **Core Trait:** Defiant, proud, and refuses to be treated as a victim. She is skilled with a rapier (`AC 15, 28 HP, +5 hit, 1d8+3 piercing, Parry Reaction +2 AC`).
* **Her Grief & Dignity:** She refuses to flee Barovia until her father receives a holy burial in consecrated church soil:
  > *"He died standing between that doorway and the wolves. I will not leave him to rot like a dead hound while I run like a coward."*
* **The Interaction:**
  * **Jacomir:** Recognizes royal courage under siege; Ireena respects Jacomir’s solemn martial bearing.
  * **Yvaine:** Examines the room’s defenses and Ireena’s bite marks. Yvaine recognizes the precision of an apex predator stalking prey.
  * **Piper:** Ireena finds comfort in Piper's familiar local face and magical spark.

---

### 🚨 The Midnight Boss: The Starved Vampire Spawn
At **3:00 AM**, the grandfather clock in the hallway tolls three heavy, metallic chimes. 
Outside, the wind abruptly dies. Dead silence falls over the estate.

> *"The third chime fades. Instantly, the candlelight in the parlor flickers and turns cold blue.
> 
> From above your heads, a heavy scratching sound begins skittering across the roof shingles. Fast. Heavy. Like an eight-foot spider dragging iron nails.
> 
> Plaster and white dust shower down into your hair.
> 
> Then—CRASH!
> 
> The stained-glass skylight in the center of the ceiling shatters into a thousand sparkling shards! A gaunt, gray-skinned humanoid silhouette lands upside-down on the exposed wooden rafters, clinging to the oak beams with blood-caked, razor-sharp claws!
> 
> It wears the shredded velvet of a Barovian magistrate. Its jaw unhinges impossibly wide, revealing rows of needle-sharp fangs dripping thick, oily black saliva. Its crimson eyes lock onto Ireena hiding behind the drawing room sofa.
> 
> It sniffs the air, letting out a wet, raspy hiss that rattles your bones:
> 
> '...The master... smells your blood, little bird... he hungers...'
> 
> It drops from the ceiling rafters straight toward her!"*

---

### ⚔️ Boss Stat Block: Starved Vampire Spawn
* **HP:** `48 HP` | **AC:** `15` | **Init:** `+3` | **Speed:** `30 ft, Spider Climb 30 ft`
* **Spider Climb:** Moves along ceilings and walls without needing an ability check.
* **Regeneration (10 HP/turn):** Heals 10 HP at the start of each of its turns **unless it takes Fire or Radiant damage** (Piper’s *Fire Bolt*, Yvaine’s phosphor/fire grenades, or blessed water shut this down!).
* **Multiattack:** Makes two attacks: one with its Claws and one Bite.
* **Claws:** `+5 to hit`, reach 5 ft, `1d6 + 3` slashing damage, and target is **Grappled** (`escape DC 13`).
* **Bite:** Only against a grappled or restrained creature. `+5 to hit`, `1d6 + 3` piercing damage + **`2d6` necrotic damage**. The target's hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the necrotic damage taken!

#### 💡 DM Tactical Advice for the Combat:
1. **Use the Ceiling:** The Spawn crawls up the parlor walls onto the ceiling to avoid Jacomir’s melee Greatsword, forcing Yvaine to shoot and Piper to blast it down.
2. **Target Ireena:** The Spawn tries to grapple Ireena. Ismark and Jacomir will naturally leap in to intercept, creating heroic frontline tension!
3. **The Fire Breakthrough:** As soon as Piper or Yvaine lands fire/radiant damage, describe the spawn’s pale flesh sizzling black, shrieking as its regeneration stops.

---

### 🕯️ THE CHILLS ENDING & CLIFFHANGER

#### 1. Slaying the Spawn
* With a final strike from Jacomir’s blade or Yvaine’s shot, the spawn lets out an ear-piercing death scream.
* Its body doesn't leave a corpse—it rapidly decays, collapsing into a heap of **powdery gray ash and blackened bone fragments** across the parlor rug.

#### 2. The Reveal of the Two Marks
* Ireena sinks to her knees beside her father's pine coffin, trembling with shock, her rapier clattering to the floorboards.
* Her high silk scarf tears loose. In the cold candlelight, the skin of her neck is fully exposed:
* **Two deep, bruised, purplish puncture wounds**—encircled by blackened veins creeping toward her collarbone.
* Ismark falls to his knees beside her, tears welling in his bloodshot eyes:
  > *"Twice he has entered this house. Twice he has taken her blood. 
  > 
  > In Barovia, the curse of the vampire is absolute: on the **third bite**, the blood stops flowing, the heart freezes... and she rises as his eternal bride."*

#### 3. The Raven on the Rafter (The Physical Table Action)
Right as Ismark finishes that sentence—
**Dead silence falls over the entire mansion.**
The wind outside abruptly dies. The rain trickles into a soundless, hanging mist.

* **DM INSTRUCTION — GO COMPLETELY SILENT. STARE AT YOUR PLAYERS. SLOWLY RAP YOUR KNUCKLES THREE TIMES ON THE TABLE:**
  > ***TAP. TAP. TAP.***

* Ismark freezes in pure terror. Ireena's breath catches in her throat.
* The sound didn't come from the heavy front door.
* It came from **above**.
* Through the shattered skylight in the ceiling, amidst the jagged glass and drizzling mist, a massive, midnight-black raven lands upon the exposed oak rafter directly above the parlor.
* It does not screech. It does not flutter. It perches motionless, tapping its black iron beak three times against the timber: *Tap. Tap. Tap.*
* Its obsidian eye catches the pale candle flame. It turns its head slowly, fixing its gaze upon each stranger in turn with terrifying, uncanny intelligence.
* And then... its throat ripples. Its beak opens.
* Out of the bird’s throat comes not a squawk, but a voice. Low. Cultured. A cold, aristocratic baritone that sounds quiet, intimate, and chillingly human—as though a nobleman were standing two inches behind their ears:

> *"A king without a crown."*
> 
> *(The raven's black eye locks onto Jacomir's battered plate armor.)*
> 
> *"A hunter blinded by blood."*
> 
> *(Its head snaps toward Yvaine, lingering on the smoking barrels of her pistols.)*
> 
> *"And a moth... fluttering toward the fire."*
> 
> *(Its gaze slides onto Piper, watching the faint magical sparks trembling at her fingertips.)*
> 
> *(The raven hops two inches forward along the rafter, its unblinking stare dropping down to Ireena, clutching her rapier beside her father's pine coffin.)*
> 
> *"You fight hard for a house that is already dead, little strays.*
> 
> *Bury your Burgomaster. Say your goodbyes.*
> 
> *When the sun sets tomorrow... she belongs to me."*

* The bird tilts its head one final time, snapping its beak with a sharp *clack*.
* From its black talons, something slips free. It tumbles silently through the cold air, landing softly with a wet rustle atop Kolyan's pine coffin, directly between the candles:
* **A single, fresh, blood-red Barovian rose.**
* With a silent, heavy sweep of its wings, the raven launches upward through the shattered skylight, disappearing into the storm clouds toward the jagged heights of Castle Ravenloft.
* In the candlelit parlor, nobody breathes. Ismark sinks to his knees, his trembling fingers clutching the silver pommel of his blade:
  > *"We have until sundown. When darkness falls tomorrow... he is coming."*

* **DM TO THE TABLE:**
  > ***"AND THAT IS WHERE WE END SESSION 1!"***
