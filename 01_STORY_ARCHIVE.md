## 🌌 PROLOGUE: THE SHATTERED SKY, THE ASFLUMPH, & THE CREEPING MISTS
* **Connection from Campaign 1 ([Dragons of Stormwreck Isle](file:///D:/Dungeons%20and%20Dragons%20Campaigns/DRAGONS%20OF%20STORMWRECK%20ISLE/00_ALWAYS_READ.md)):**
  * Following the defeat of the blue dragon **Sparkrender** atop the Clifftop Observatory, **Eflein**, **Sylvar Link**, **Father Flubs**, **General Varnoth**, and **Krag'zor** placed all three Metallic Dragon Scales back into the dragon statues to activate the **Draconic Star-Atlas**, revealing the hidden coordinates across the western ocean to the **Lost Dragon Lands** (where **Lianna** and **Lyra** sailed).
  * After an emotional farewell—where **Thurible** gifted petals from his flower crown to Eflein and Sylvar, **Father Flubs** gifted Sylvar a small bottomless bottle of his sacred wine, and Sylvar gifted Flubs a pendant of his fur—**Father Flubs and Thurible** remained behind as Sworn Defenders of Sharruth's Seal.
  * As **Eflein and Sylvar** prepared to sail across the sea aboard their flagship ***The Asflumph*** (named in Flubs's honor), the tavern candles froze into blue icicles and **Vecna's weeping Obsidian Monoliths** and swarms of vampires tore through the sky!
* **The Shadow Demiplane of Barovia:**
  * As the planar boundaries between worlds frayed, the ancient **Mists of Ravenloft** reached out across distant lands and backstories—snatching four complete strangers at **Level 3** and dragging them into the domain of **Count Strahd von Zarovich**.
  * Unknown to these new travelers, their journey through Barovia—and the forbidden bargains waiting for them in the **Amber Temple**—will forge them into the very champions whom Eflein and Sylvar must one day face in **Campaign 3**!

## 📅 SESSION LOGS & CAMPAIGN CHRONICLE

### 🩸 Session 1: "Blood on the Axle & The Man Between Seconds" *(Completed — Level 3)*
* **Full Session Script Reference:** [`Session 1 - Blood on the Axle (The Svalich Woods Carriage Chase).md`](file:///D:/Dungeons%20and%20Dragons%20Campaigns/CURSE%20OF%20STRAHD/Session%201%20-%20Blood%20on%20the%20Axle%20(The%20Svalich%20Woods%20Carriage%20Chase).md)
* **Actual Session 1 Events & Canonical Improvs:**
  * **1. The Svalich Woods Carriage Chase & Gates of Barovia:** Plucked from their home worlds by the choking Mists, **King Jacomir Yulle**, **Eclipsa Rosaline Yvaine**, and **Piper the Incomparable** survived the runaway **Phantom Black Stagecoach** wolf ambush, crashed before the **Gates of Barovia**, found **Dalvan Olensky's** corpse with the spinning compass, and entered the mist-locked valley.
  * **2. Morgantha ("Granny") & Jacomir's Uneaten Dream Pastry:** On the road into the village, the party met old **Morgantha** peddling her warm **Dream Pastries**. **Piper** attempted to cast her innate Fierna *Charm Person* on the old woman, but Morgantha (secretly a Night Hag with *Magic Resistance*) resisted the spell and became semi-hostile and wary toward Piper. Before departing into the mist, Morgantha handed **1x Dream Pastry to King Jacomir** for him to keep—which Jacomir stored in his inventory **uneaten**.
  * **3. Blood on the Vine Tavern & Piper's Blank Tarokka Card:** Inside the tavern, the Vistani stared at **Piper** with eyes full of concern, warning her to stay safe because they saw deep misfortune and darkness behind her eyes. When one Vistani drew a card for her, **the painted ink faded right off the parchment**, leaving **🃏 A Blank Tarokka Card**! They gave the Blank Card to Piper and instructed her to bring it to **Madam Eva** at the **Tser Pool Encampment**.
  * **4. Looting Indirovich Manor & Burgomaster Kolyan's Wizard Diary:** Meeting **Ismark Kolyanovich** and his sister **Ireena Kolyana**, the party searched and looted Indirovich Manor. They discovered that the late Burgomaster **Kolyan Indirovich** was a former wizard/scholar and found **📖 Burgomaster Kolyan's Wizard Diary**, wherein he wrote that he knows **Davian Martikov** and **the Keepers of the Feather**, who can help with the Strahd situation if the party needs further assistance!
  * **5. The Barovian Church Attic & The Doru Moral Dilemma (`DragnaCarta Reloaded Arc B5`):** At the village church, the party discovered **Father Donavich** hiding his vampire spawn son **Doru** in the **church attic**. A tense moral standoff erupted:
    * **Father Donavich** desperately wanted to keep his son alive despite Doru being a vampire spawn and traitor.
    * **King Jacomir** and **Ismark** empathized with a father's grief (while agreeing it is wrong to keep Doru chained like this), and **Jacomir began pouring his own royal blood into a cup** to give Father Donavich to feed Doru and briefly restore his lucidity.
    * **Piper** and **Yvaine** fiercely disagreed, demanding they kill the vampire spawn immediately, while **Ireena** remained neutral.
  * **6. Session 1 Cliffhanger — `Tap, Tap, Tap` (Derek, Avatar of the Dark Powers):** Right at the peak of the moral dilemma inside the church, three knocks sounded (`tap, tap, tap`)—and **time froze completely**. As Donavich, Ismark, and Ireena stood frozen like statues, a tall white man named **Derek**—an **Avatar / Emissary of the Dark Powers**—stepped into the church:
    * Speaking first to **Jacomir** and **Yvaine**, Derek revealed he knows exactly who they are and where they come from, promising that if they continue their journey and **kill Strahd**, they shall get what they want and go home.
    * Turning his gaze to **Piper**, Derek paused in curiosity and astonishment—realizing *what she is* (the chaotic Wild Magic conduit/vessel) and what plans the Dark Powers have for her—and told her to *"take care of herself."*
    * Telling them to follow him as their ally and slay Strahd, Derek vanished—**unfreezing time** and dropping Jacomir, Yvaine, and Piper right back into the middle of the Doru blood-cup moral dilemma to close Session 1!

## 🏛️ Major Lore & Cosmic Secrets Discovered (DM Tracking)
* **The Cosmic Hierarchy (Avatar vs. Scion vs. Vessels):**
  * **Derek ("The Man Between Seconds" — Avatar of the Dark Powers):** Operates strictly inside frozen pockets of time (`tap, tap, tap`), meaning **Count Strahd has zero knowledge of Derek's existence** (bats, wolves, and scrying cannot perceive what happens between the ticks of the clock). Appears only at **3 future Milestone Crossroads** (Post-Tarokka at Tser Falls, the Amber Temple, and Ravenloft Gates) to push ruthless, pragmatic choices.
  * **Caelum Jude ("Cael" — The Scion / Inside Shepherd):** The mortal-born Circle of Stars Druid companion who walks with the party in real time, heals them, and loves them genuinely—while shrinking back in silent terror whenever Derek's frozen time-stops occur.
  * **Piper's Blank Tarokka Card (Option 2 — 3-Stage Living Omen):** Madam Eva cannot read Piper's ending directly; instead, the Blank Card bleeds charcoal ink in **3 progressive stages** across the campaign (*Stage 1: Dead pines & empty upturned hands rising from black water* ➔ *Stage 2: A cracked, faceless glass lantern/pocket watch drinking in silver fog* ➔ *Stage 3: A violent vortex of endless Mist pouring inward into the cracked glass*).
* **Burgomaster Kolyan's Wizard Diary (*Keepers of the Feather Bridge*):** Directly connects the party to **Urwin Martikov** (*Blue Water Inn*, Vallaki) and **Davian Martikov** (*Wizard of Wines* Winery).
