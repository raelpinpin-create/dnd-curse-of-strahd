   * **Cinematic Action Engines:** High-adrenaline rules (like the *"One Success Keeps You Moving"* chase engine) with individual complications instead of game-stalling failures.

   * **Physical Table Cues & Chills Endings:** Verbatim instructions for in-person table actions (knocking on tables, dimming lights, physical props) ending on an unforgettable cliffhanger with a strict in-world ticking clock.

## ⚖️ Table House Rules & Barovian Gothic Laws
* **Brutal Critical Hits:** Max Base Die + 1 Rolled Die + Modifier.
* **Death Saves (2-Strike / 1-Success Rule):** 1 success stabilizes at 1 HP (prone); 2 failures = Permanent Death *(or in Barovia: a whispered offer of resurrection from the Dark Powers with an permanent Gothic Flaw/Corruption!)*.
* **Potion of Healing:** **Bonus Action** self (`2d6 + 1 HP`) | **Action** administer to ally.
* **Short Rest Healing (Max 2/day):** Restores **25% of Max HP** and regains **+1 Help Token** (max 2/2).
* **Help Tokens (2/2 Max):** Free call-out within 30 ft for `+1d6` to an ally's roll (requires narrative justification).
* **Barovian Mist Law:** Attempting to leave Barovia through the border Mists forces a DC 15 CON save each minute against 1 level of Exhaustion and always turns the traveler back around into the valley.
* **Barovian Sunlight Law:** Barovia's natural daylight is perpetually muted by thick storm clouds and mist—it does **not** count as true sunlight for vampire hypersensitivity (only *The Sunsword*, *The Holy Symbol of Ravenkind*, and the *Dawn* spell produce true Barovian sunlight!).
