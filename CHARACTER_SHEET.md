Terrified of being seen as a weapon of mass destruction, Piper refused to use real magic, touring as *"Piper the Incomparable"* with fake props. Then came the vivid nightmare of her parents fending off chaos in the Svalich Woods—and suddenly her suppressed Fierna Wild Magic violently awakened! Dropping her watch in the fog, she snapped her fingers for a fake fire trick and accidentally detonated a real spectral inferno. Piper currently thinks nothing of *how* or *why* her powers abruptly awakened, assuming it was merely panic and adrenaline. She is completely unaware that she was **handpicked as one of the prophesied chosen by the Dark Powers themselves**, selected for her rare, innate genius for channeling reality-bending chaotic magic. As the only **native of Barovia** in the party, she desperately wants her magic to bring joy to this gloomy realm and seeks to find her lost parents—unaware that every chaotic surge draws her closer to Castle Ravenloft and the Amber Temple, where the Dark Powers intend to bind her as Vecna's living anchor!
