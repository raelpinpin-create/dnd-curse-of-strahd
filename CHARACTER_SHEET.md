* **Spellcasting (Charisma):** **Spell Save DC:** `14` | **Spell Attack Bonus:** `+6`
* **Ability Scores (5.5e Statgen):** `STR 11 (+0) | DEX 15 (+2) | CON 13 (+1) | INT 12 (+1) | WIS 11 (+0) | CHA 18 (+4) ★`

* **Class & Lineage Resources:**
  - ✨ **Sorcery Points (`Font of Magic`):** `3 / 3` *(Long Rest)* — **Metamagic:** *Subtle Spell (1 SP)*, *Twinned Spell (1–2 SP)*, *Bend Fate ("Is That Your Card?!" — 2 SP for ±1d4 to a roll)*
  - 🃏 **Tides of Chaos ("Pick-a-Card, Any Card!"):** `1 / 1 Ready` *(Gain Advantage on 1 Attack/Check/Save; once spent, her next 1st+ level spell automatically triggers a d100 Wild Magic Surge and recharges Tides of Chaos!)*
  - 💖 **Legacy of Phlegethos (Fierna Lineage):** Knows *Friends* cantrip + **1/Long Rest Free 2nd-Level *Charm Person*** (targets up to 2 humanoids)
  - 🔥 **Hellish Resistance & Darkvision:** **Resistance to Fire Damage** • **Darkvision 60 ft**
  - 🤝 **Help Tokens:** `2 / 2`
* **Spell Slots & Known Spells:**
  - **1st-Level Slots (`4 / 4`):** *Chaos Bolt* (`+6 hit`, `2d8+1d6` elemental + Chaos Leap on matching d8s), *Mage Armor* (`15 AC`), *Charm Person* (`DC 14 WIS`)
  - **2nd-Level Slots (`2 / 2`):** *Web* (`DC 14 DEX` Restrained), *Misty Step* (`30ft Bonus Action teleport`), *Charm Person (Fierna 2nd-Lvl)*
  - **Cantrips (At-Will):** *Fire Bolt* (`+6 hit`, `1d10` Fire), *Friends*, *Prestidigitation*, *Minor Illusion* (`DC 14`), *Mage Hand*
* **Equipment & Inventory (`15 GP, 0 SP`):**
  - 🕰️ *Barnaby's Brass Pocket Watch* (Blurred portrait of her purple-horned tiefling parents)
  - 🎩 *False-Bottom Stage Props & Rigged Card Deck*
  - 🪄 *Quarterstaff* (Magician's Cane & Arcane Focus • `+2 hit`, `1d6/1d8` bludgeoning)
  - 🗡️ *2x Daggers* (`+4 hit`, `1d4+2` piercing, thrown 20/60 ft)
  - 👝 *Component Pouch* & 🎒 *Explorer's Pack* (`10x Torches`, `10x Rations`, `Waterskin`, `50ft Rope`)
* **Origin Lore & Svalich Nightmare Hook:** Raised in a cold elvish orphanage and shunned for her purple horns, Piper was mentored by an old magician named **Barnaby**, who gifted her a brass pocket watch holding a blurred portrait of a tiefling couple (*"Only you can find them, O prophesied one"*). Refusing to ever use real magic so no one would call her an infernal monster, she toured as *"Piper the Incomparable"* using fake stage props. Lured to the foggy Svalich Woods by a nightmare of her parents fighting a shadowy horror, she dropped her watch in the dark, snapped her fingers for her fake thumb-fire trick—and Barovia's dark magic ignited her suppressed Wild Magic into a blast of spectral hellfire. Now every "stage trick" she performs unleashes real, explosive magic that she frantically insists is *"All part of the act!"*

* **Saving Throws:** **STR ★ `+5`**, **CON ★ `+5`**, WIS `+2`, DEX `+0`, INT `+0`, CHA `+0`
* **Proficient Skills:** **Athletics (STR) ★ `+5`**, **Insight (WIS) ★ `+4`**, **Perception (WIS) ★ `+4`** *(Human: Skillful)*, **Survival (WIS) ★ `+4`** *(Skilled)*, **History (INT) ★ `+2`** *(Noble)*, **Persuasion (CHA) ★ `+2`** *(Noble)*, **Intimidation (CHA) ★ `+2`** *(Skilled)* • **Tool Proficiency:** *Dragonchess Set*
* **Weapons & 5.5e Weapon Masteries:**
  - 🗡️ **Ancestral Greatsword:** `+5 to hit`, `2d6 + 3` Slashing *(Savage Attacker: rolls 2d6 twice 1/turn and uses higher total)* • **Graze Mastery:** Deals **3 Slashing damage** (STR mod) even on a missed attack roll!
  - 🪓 **2x Handaxes (Melee or Thrown 20/60 ft):** `+5 to hit`, `1d6 + 3` Slashing • **Vex Mastery:** Hitting grants **Advantage** on the next attack roll against that target before the end of your next turn!
* **Battle Master Combat Superiority (`Maneuver Save DC 13`):**
  - ⚔️ **Superiority Dice:** `4 / 4 (d8s)` *(Recharges on Short or Long Rest)*
  - 🛡️ **Parry (Reaction):** When damaged by a melee attack, expend 1 Superiority Die to reduce damage by `1d8 + 3` (STR).
  - ⚔️ **Riposte (Reaction):** When a creature misses you with a melee attack, expend 1 Superiority Die to make an immediate melee weapon attack (`+5 hit`, adds `+1d8` to damage on hit).
  - 👹 **Menacing Attack (On Hit):** Expend 1 Superiority Die on a weapon hit to add `+1d8` damage and force a **DC 13 WIS Save** or the target is **Frightened** of you until the end of your next turn.
* **Core Fighter Features & 5.5e Human Origin Feats:**
  - 💨 **Second Wind (`3 / 3` uses per Long Rest, `+1` on Short Rest):** Bonus Action to heal `1d10 + 3 HP`.
  - 🧠 **Tactical Mind:** When you fail an ability check, expend 1 use of Second Wind to add `+1d10` to the check *(if it still fails, the use is NOT expended!)*.
  - ⚡ **Action Surge (`1 / 1` per Short or Long Rest):** Take 1 additional Action on your turn.
  - 🌟 **Heroic Inspiration (`Human: Resourceful`):** Regained on every Long Rest; reroll any die immediately after rolling it.
  - 🩸 **Origin Feats:** **Savage Attacker** *(Human Versatile)* & **Skilled** *(Noble Background)*.
  - 🤝 **Help Tokens:** `2 / 2`
* **Equipment & Royal Regalia (`25 GP, 0 SP`):**
  - 🗡️ *Ancestral Greatsword*, 🪓 *2x Handaxes*, ⛓️ *Chain Mail (AC 16)*
  - 💍 *Signet Ring bearing the Lost Crest of Oakenshire*, 🧥 *Tattered Royal Traveling Cloak with Fur Mantle*, 📜 *Empty Scroll Case*
  - ♟️ *Dragonchess Gaming Set*, 👔 *Fine Traveler's Garments*, 🎒 *Explorer's Pack*
* **Origin Lore & Roleplay Traits:** Former King of **Oakenshire** whose realm and vanguard were swallowed overnight by the creeping Mists. He carries himself as an anointed sovereign who will bleed for his companions as his royal vanguard, sworn to tear Barovia apart stone by stone to find his lost people.
